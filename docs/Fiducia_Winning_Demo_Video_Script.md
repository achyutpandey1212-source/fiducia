# Fiducia — Winning 4-Minute Demo Video Pitch Script

**Target Duration:** ~4 Minutes (3:45 – 4:15 at a natural, confident speaking pace)  
**Copywriting Framework:** **PAS** (Problem $\rightarrow$ Agitate $\rightarrow$ Solution) + **BAB** (Before $\rightarrow$ After $\rightarrow$ Bridge)  
**Target Audience:** Hackathon Judges / Investment Committee / Technical Evaluators  

---

## 0. How Winning Teams Film Their Demo (Quick Recording Rules)

1. **Pre-Setup Before Pressing Record:**
   - Open your browser to the **Fiducia Landing Page** (`/`) in full-screen (`F11` or hide bookmarks bar).
   - Ensure you are **Signed Out** initially so you can smoothly show the `/login` $\rightarrow$ `Continue as Guest Adviser` flow, **OR** if already signed in, ensure your workspace is cleared (`Clear All Folios` inside the bottom-left account menu) so the workbench starts completely clean.
2. **Pacing & Tone:**
   - **0:00 – 0:50 (The Hook & Problem):** Speak slightly slower, punchy, and serious. Make the judges feel the ₹42 Lakh pain of commission-driven banking.
   - **0:50 – 3:30 (The Product Walkthrough):** Energetic, crisp, and technical. Every sentence you speak must correspond to a mouse movement or click on screen.
   - **3:30 – 4:10 (The Proof & Close):** Authoritative and memorable.
